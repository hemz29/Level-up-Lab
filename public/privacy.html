Privacy notice

Level-Up Lab · last updated November 2026

Level-Up Lab is a study-habit coach for school children. It doesn't teach content, show ads, sell data, or use AI. This notice explains what we keep and why, in plain words.

Who signs up

Only a parent or guardian can create an account, using their own email address. Children don't need an email address, phone number or password. The parent adds each child with a first name or nickname only.

We don't collect location, contacts, photos of the child, school names, or advertising identifiers. There are no third-party analytics or ad trackers.

Services we use

  • Supabase: secure database and sign-in.
  • Vercel: hosts the app.
  • Resend: sends sign-in codes and the daily report email.

They process data only to provide these services to us.

Keeping it safe

Data is encrypted in transit. Database rules mean each family can only ever reach its own children's data. The team's dashboard receives numbers only, never children's notes or drawings.

Your choices

  • Delete a child's profile or your whole account at any time in Parent → Account. Deletion is immediate and permanent.
  • Download a copy of your child's data in Parent → Data.
  • The daily report is only sent when the child presses the button, after seeing exactly what it says.
